Our practice is founded and led by Er. Ajay Gupta, Civil Engineering graduate from IIT Roorkee (1989). He is a Fellow of the Institution of Engineers (India) and Institution of Valuers India, Chairman of APGCE (India), and a recognized Chartered Engineer and KDA Government-Approved Technical Consultant.

We conduct non-destructive testing (NDT), load capacity audits, and issue statutory stability certificates accepted by factory directorates, labor departments, and industrial licensing authorities.
We treat Vastu as the science of orientation, solar energy, and natural airflow. Our layout diagrams harmonize elemental zones (Brahmasthan, Ishanya, Agneya) with structural column grids and shear walls so you receive 100% Vastu compliance without compromising seismic safety or structural beauty.
