ABOUT NIRMAN CONSULTANTS

Architectural Brilliance & Structural Precision Since 1991

Nirman Consultants, headquartered in Kanpur and founded by Er. Ajay Gupta (IIT Roorkee, 1989), is an apex architectural and structural design consultancy. With over three decades of professional practice, our firm has successfully designed and delivered 800+ landmark projects across 40+ Indian cities.

Our multidisciplinary practice spans residential masterplans, high-rise commercial complexes, industrial plants, bank institutional buildings, and factories. We integrate 100% Vastu Shastra harmony with strict BIS seismic codes (IS 1893, IS 456, IS 13920) and National Building Code (NBC 2016) compliance.

WORK PROCESS

Disciplined 6-Stage
Engineering Lifecycle

Site Feasibility & Vastu

01

Comprehensive topographical study, zoning evaluation, solar orientation, and authentic Vastu Shastra space alignment.

3D Architectural Modeling

02

Interactive 3D visualization, BIM elevation schematics, functional daylighting design, and municipal sanction drawings.

IS Structural Design

03

STAAD.Pro & ETABS computational modelling per IS 1893 seismic parameters, IS 456 concrete and IS 13920 ductile detailing.

Regulatory Approvals

04

Government sanction documentation, KDA municipal clearance, fire safety compliance, and NBC 2016 statutory filings.

PMC & Site Supervision

05

On-site quality assurance, bar-bending schedules, material non-destructive testing, and contractor coordination.

Handover & Stability Audit

06

Turnkey commissioning, as-built documentation, and legal structural stability certification under the Factories Act, 1948.
