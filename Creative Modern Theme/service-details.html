Flagship Engineering Discipline

Structure Designing (RCC Frame & PEB)

Under the direct guidance of Er. Ajay Gupta (Alumnus, IIT Roorkee 1989), Nirman Consultants provides industry-leading structural analysis, peer vetting, and architectural engineering across multi-storey residential towers, institutional universities, and industrial heavy spans throughout 40+ Indian cities.

Every blueprint undergoes strict mathematical simulation in accordance with IS 456:2000 (Plain and Reinforced Concrete), IS 1893:2016 (Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures), IS 875 (Wind & Imposed Loads), and the National Building Code (NBC 2016). Our integrated 3D Building Information Modelling (BIM) guarantees that structural frameworks blend harmoniously with mechanical, electrical, and aesthetic elements.

Key Scope & Deliverables

  • Complete structural framing & rebar scheduling
  • Analysis of PEB steel industrial warehouses & gantry girders
  • Seismic & wind load modeling under IS 1893:2016 & IS 875
  • Factory Stability Certification under Section 6 of Factories Act, 1948
Bureau of Indian Standards

100% compliance with IS 456, IS 800, IS 1893, IS 13920 (Ductile Detailing), and NBC 2016 for municipal sanctions & legal stability certification.

BIM & Clash Coordination

Full dimensional synchronization eliminating clashes between MEP ducts, plumbing stacks, and load-bearing concrete framing before execution.

Our 4-Stage Project Lifecycle

We follow an empirical engineering roadmap that bridges design intent with ground reality:

  • Phase 1: Conceptual Vetting & Geotechnical Review – Analyzing soil profiles, SBC records, setback norms, and architectural footprints.
  • Phase 2: Mathematical Simulation & Stress Analysis – STAAD.Pro / ETABS 3D computational model under dead, live, wind, and seismic load combinations.
  • Phase 3: Good-For-Construction (GFC) Blueprints – Millimeter-accurate column schedules, slab rebar drawings, foundation details, and BBS tables.
  • Phase 4: Site Audits & Shuttering Inspections – On-ground rebar tie checks before concrete pours, cube strength testing reviews, and stability certification.
