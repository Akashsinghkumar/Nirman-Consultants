About Nirman Consultants

We Design With You In Mind
Since 1991

Nirman Consultants, based in Kanpur and founded in 1991, specializes in turning paper sketches into incredible spaces for living, working, and learning. As a leading building design consultancy, our dedicated team combines experience across architecture, civil, and structural engineering to deliver unique, cost-effective projects ahead of schedule.

Vastu Compliant & IS/NBC Certified

Every blueprint integrates customized client brief, Vastu alignment, and strict adherence to Indian Standards (IS Code) & National Building Code (NBC).

Multi-Disciplinary Engineering

RCC Frame analysis, PEB design, fire safety compliance, HVAC coordination, and turnkey interior management under one roof.
