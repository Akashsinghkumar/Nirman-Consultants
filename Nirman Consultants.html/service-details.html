Structure Designing (RCC Frame & PEB)

Under the direct guidance of Ajay Gupta (Civil Engineer, IIT Roorkee 1989), our structural engineering team focuses on state-of-the-art analysis and design of RCC Frame and PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) structures strictly in accordance with Indian Standards (IS Code).

Key Deliverables
  • Complete structural framing plan
  • Rebar & column beam reinforcement details
  • PEB steel connection diagrams
  • Foundation & soil bearing analysis
Statutory Adherence
  • IS 456: Plain & Reinforced Concrete
  • IS 800: Structural Steel Construction
  • IS 1893: Earthquake Resistant Design
  • National Building Code (NBC) 2016
